Personal Project Astro / Supabase

Keys of Life

Keys of Life was born from the desire to link naturalist expertise with strict data organization. The core of the project relies on the design of an interconnected database to map the flora and fauna of France. Thanks to this rigorous structuring, the system dynamically generates identification keys and interactive phylogenetic trees, offering a reliable and high-performance exploration tool. Technically, taxa and their relationships are managed in a relational database (Supabase/NocoDB). The site then relies on a recursive algorithm that traverses this flat data in real time to reconstruct and visually model the hierarchy of living things.

Astro, HTML & GitHub Relational databases Flora/Fauna Taxonomy
